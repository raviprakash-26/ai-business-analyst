# Demo Dataset

`demo_retail_business.csv` is a synthetic Indian retail dataset designed for local demonstrations of the AI Business Analyst platform.

## Coverage
- 30 orders
- 30 customers
- January–June 2026
- South, West, North, and East regions
- Electronics and Furniture
- Revenue, cost, profit, quantity, discounts
- UPI, card, cash, and net-banking payments

## Safety
All names/IDs are synthetic. The dataset contains no real customer personal information.

## Suggested questions
- What is total revenue?
- Which region has the highest revenue?
- Which product contributes most to profit?
- What is the revenue trend?
- Are there any unusual values?
- What should management investigate?
